# Ex.No.10 – Content Creation: Reports, Articles, and More Using Prompt Patterns

**Date:** 13/11/25

## Aim

To demonstrate how various prompting techniques—query decomposition, decision-making, semantic filtering, etc.—can be employed to create business reports, articles, case studies, or creative works with ChatGPT or similar models. The objective is to highlight how different prompt structures affect the quality, coherence, and structure of generated content. [file:2]

---

## Apparatus / Software Required

- ChatGPT or equivalent AI-based content generation model (Gemini, Copilot, Claude, etc.)
- Internet-connected computer or laptop [file:2]

---

## Theory

Prompt engineering is the process of designing effective queries (prompts) that guide large language models to generate purposeful, accurate responses. Quality, detail, and structure of prompts directly influence clarity, creativity, and factual reliability of content. [file:2]

**Key Prompt Patterns:**
1. **Query Decomposition:** Break complex tasks into smaller, manageable sub-tasks.
2. **Decision Making:** Ask the model to choose ideas for a focused direction.
3. **Answer Engineering:** Structure output—headings, tone, bullet points.
4. **Fact Check List:** Ensure factual accuracy and credibility.
5. **Tail Generation:** Extend content logically for depth.
6. **Menu Actions:** Offer multiple content paths for selective focus.
7. **Semantic Filtering:** Adjust style, tone, and precision using descriptor filters. [file:2]

---

## Procedure

1. **Introduction to Prompt Patterns:**  
   Study logic and purpose behind each pattern.
2. **Selection of Content Type:**  
   (Business Report, Case Study, Article, Creative Writing, etc.)
3. **Test Case Scenario Selection:**  
   Example scenarios:
   - Business Report on Market Trends
   - Creative Writing: Sci-Fi Short Story
4. **Creating Basic Prompts:**  
   Develop an initial, simple prompt.
5. **Applying Advanced Prompt Patterns:**  
   Refine with Decision Making, Tail Generation, Semantic Filtering, etc.
6. **Generating First and Refined Versions:**  
   Produce successive drafts, improving prompts.
7. **Evaluation of Outputs:**  
   Assess coherence, clarity, creativity, engagement, accuracy, tone.
8. **Iteration and Finalization:**  
   Select and document the best version. [file:2]

## Observations

- Structured prompting (decomposition, semantic filtering, tail generation) significantly improves quality, coherence, creativity, and tone of AI-generated outputs.
- Successive refinement by breaking down tasks, guiding decisions, and extending narratives produces more useful content than simple prompts. [file:2]

---

## Result

Experiment demonstrates that advanced prompt engineering (query decomposition, decision making, semantic filtering) enhances AI-generated content quality and structure.
