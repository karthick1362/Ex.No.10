# Ex.No.10
Content Creation (Reports, Articles, Case Studies, etc.) Using Prompt Patterns

## Date: 13/11/25
## Reg. No. 212223053002

## Aim:
To demonstrate how various prompting techniques (query decomposition, decision-making, semantic filtering, etc.) can be employed to create content such as reports, articles, case studies, or creative works like comic books, using ChatGPT or similar models. The objective is to highlight how different prompt structures affect the content's quality, coherence, and structure.
---

## Apparatus / Software Required
- ChatGPT or any equivalent AI-based content generation model (e.g., Gemini, Copilot, Claude)
- Internet-connected computer or laptop

---

## Theory
Prompt engineering is the process of designing effective queries (prompts) that guide large language models to generate purposeful and accurate responses.  
The quality, detail, and structure of prompts directly influence the clarity, creativity, and factual reliability of generated content.

### Key Prompt Patterns
1. **Query Decomposition** – Breaking complex tasks into smaller, manageable sub-tasks.  
2. **Decision Making** – Asking the model to choose between ideas, helping define a focused direction.  
3. **Answer Engineering** – Structuring output (e.g., adding headings, tone, or bullet points).  
4. **Fact Check List** – Ensuring factual accuracy and credibility in content.  
5. **Tail Generation** – Extending content logically to build narrative depth.  
6. **Menu Actions** – Offering multiple content paths for selective focus.  
7. **Semantic Filtering** – Adjusting style, tone, and precision using descriptor filters.

---

## Procedure
1. **Introduction to Prompt Patterns**  
   Study and understand the purpose and design logic behind each type of prompt.

2. **Selection of Content Type**  
   Choose the desired form of content from:  
   - Business Report  
   - Case Study  
   - Article (Educational or Opinion-based)  
   - Creative Writing (Story, Script, or Dialogue)

3. **Test Case Scenario Selection**  
   For this experiment:  
   - **Scenario 1:** Business Report on Market Trends  
   - **Scenario 2:** Creative Writing – Sci-Fi Short Story

4. **Creating Basic Prompts**  
   Start with a general prompt to generate an initial simple version.

5. **Applying Advanced Prompt Patterns**  
   Refine prompts using techniques like Decision Making, Tail Generation, and Semantic Filtering.

6. **Generating First and Refined Versions**  
   Produce successive versions and modify prompts for better structure and focus.

7. **Evaluation of Outputs**  
   Assess each version for:  
   - Coherence and Clarity  
   - Creativity and Engagement  
   - Accuracy and Tone Alignment  

8. **Iteration and Finalization**  
   Choose the final version after iterative refinement and improvement.

## Test Case 1: Business Report on Market Trends

### Basic Prompt
Write a brief business report on current global market trends.

text

### First Draft Output
The global market in 2025 is shaped by technological innovation, sustainability efforts, and post-pandemic recovery.  
Digital transformation across fintech, electric vehicles, and e-commerce drives growth.  
Asian markets lead in manufacturing, while North America dominates AI and green technology investments.

### Refined Prompt (Using Query Decomposition and Decision Making)
Generate a detailed business report analyzing the 2025 global market trends. Focus on three major sectors: technology, renewable energy, and e-commerce. Present insights in a formal business tone with subheadings and bullet points.

text

### Refined Output
**Introduction:**  
The 2025 global market shows significant structural changes due to advancing technology and global sustainability initiatives.

**Technology Sector:**  
Artificial intelligence and automation remain central drivers, boosting global productivity.

**Renewable Energy:**  
Growing investment in solar, wind, and hydrogen technologies marks a shift toward carbon neutrality.

**E-Commerce:**  
Online marketplaces expand steadily as logistics and consumer convenience improve.

**Conclusion:**  
A digital-first, eco-conscious economy continues to shape the global market landscape.

## Test Case 2: Creative Writing – Sci-Fi Short Story

### Basic Prompt
Write a short science fiction story set in the future.

text

### First Draft Output
In 2140, humans colonized Mars. A young engineer discovers a radio signal coming from beneath the Martian surface.

### Refined Prompt (Using Tail Generation and Semantic Filtering)
Expand the short sci-fi story into a cinematic narrative. Describe the setting, main character (an engineer named Aria), and her discovery of a hidden civilization beneath Mars’s surface. Maintain a vivid, engaging tone.

text

### Refined Output
Aria, a systems engineer at the Red Horizon colony, begins hearing mysterious signals echoing through her comm system.  
Following their source, she ventures into a network of glowing tunnels beneath the Martian crust, illuminated by bioluminescent crystals.  
There, she encounters remnants of an ancient Martian civilization preserved through nanotechnology.  
The transmission, she learns, was never a distress call—it was a plea for remembrance.

## Result
The experiment demonstrates that structured prompting techniques—such as Query Decomposition, Decision Making, and Semantic Filtering—significantly enhance AI-generated content.  
Outputs improved across coherence, creativity, and tone by progressively refining prompt structures.
